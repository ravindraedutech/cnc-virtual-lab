CNC Virtual Lab
A browser-based CNC lathe and milling simulator for learning FANUC-style G-code and APT programming.
Open the lab: https://ravindraedutech.github.io/cnc-virtual-lab/
Runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.
CNC lathe and vertical milling machine, with 3D, top and section views.
36 exercises (lathe 20, mill 16) with starter programs, solutions and automatic part checking.
FANUC 0i-style lathe canned cycles: G70, G71, G72, G73, G74, G75, G76, G90, G92, G94, and subprograms (M98/M99).
Drilling, boring and parting off on the lathe.
G-code and APT, analysis, bill of material, worksheets, viva questions and examinations.
Companion to the book CNC Programming with CNC Virtual Lab and the video series
CNC Programming Masterclass on the InnoSphere EduTech YouTube channel.
Your data
Accounts, progress and records are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
